# Gill Hand

Original signature-inspired typeface created for Gill Brophy’s portfolio.
Uppercase, lowercase, digits, punctuation and navigation symbols.

The alphabet interprets the narrow lettering, tall loops and forward lean of
Gill’s signature on Hellebores. It is not a facsimile of her full handwriting;
letters not present in that signature were designed for this typeface.
The top-left logo remains a direct photographic crop of her real signature.

Rebuild with Python and fonttools: `python scripts/build_handwriting.py`.
No third-party font outlines are used.
