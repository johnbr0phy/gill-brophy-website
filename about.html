The familiar,
seen afresh.

Gill Brophy’s paintings bring flowers, still life and landscape into a world of vivid colour, expressive lines and layered patterns.

Tulips become sweeping shapes. Pots and bottles form playful arrangements. Branches, gardens and familiar places open into something unexpected.

Look closely and there is more to discover: a line winding through a flower, a pattern within a vessel, the meeting of one colour with another.
