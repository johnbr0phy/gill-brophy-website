About the artist

Gill Brophy

Flowers, familiar places and everyday objects, painted with a love of colour.

Gill’s work ranges from tulips and garden flowers to landscapes, still lifes and characterful animals. Bold colour and expressive lines run through the collection, bringing a playful quality to familiar subjects.

Some paintings are richly textured; others use simple shapes and delicate marks. Across them all, there’s a close attention to pattern, movement and the small details that give each subject its character.
